Felipe Meneguitti Dias

São Paulo, Brazil · +55 (32) 99173-4060

I'm an electronics engineer with a PhD from the University of São Paulo. I work as a Senior R&D Specialist at Samsung in Campinas, Brazil.

Before joining Samsung, I worked on biomedical signals and healthcare AI at InCor. My PhD focused on ECG image classification. I also developed software and worked with clinicians to integrate the models into the emergency department's workflow.

Heart Institute (InCor), HCFMUSP

Researcher / Senior Systems Analyst, Healthcare AI
2020–2026 · São Paulo, Brazil

  • Developed models to classify ECG images and helped integrate the classifier into the emergency department. The evaluation included cardiologist review and comparison with specialists.
  • Coordinated the annotation of around 20,000 ECGs by 19 cardiologists, from defining the classes to reviewing conflicting readings and analyzing agreement.
  • Led AIMED in the 2024 PhysioNet Challenge, where we placed first in the classification task. First author of the paper describing our method.
  • Led Gorgona and built the initial integration between urgent care units in Salvador, the InCor server and Telegram. The system alerts the referral coordination team when an ECG suggests ST-elevation myocardial infarction. ECGs also go through telemedicine review.
  • Developed protocols for collecting signals from watches in RPMS. Worked with the team on photoplethysmography (PPG) processing and algorithms for estimating physiological parameters.
  • Developed and evaluated language models to anonymize clinical text in Portuguese. Prepared an annotated dataset, compared models and worked on local deployment to protect the data.
